Twitch chat voting. Type your Twitch channel name in the text field and press Enter; you hear when the chat is connected. The interval and maximum votes lists set how often a vote runs and how many options it offers, and the check boxes turn single vote options on or off. Each new vote is announced with the numbers viewers type in chat, for example #2. {Status} shows the running vote counts and the time left, Enter on that line opens or closes these settings.
